Step by step How to create and use a QR code Four short steps from idea to print-ready code
  1. 1

    Pick your content type

    Choose a website link, plain text, a Wi-Fi network, an email address, or a phone number. Each type builds a slightly different payload, so pick the one that matches what you want a scan to actually do.

  2. 2

    Enter the details carefully

    Type the information exactly as it should behave. A typo in a URL or a Wi-Fi password gets baked into the code, and every scanner will faithfully reproduce the mistake.

  3. 3

    Customize the design

    Adjust colors, module shapes, and optional center artwork. Keep strong contrast between the pattern and the background, and always leave a clear quiet zone around the edges.

  4. 4

    Download and scan-test

    Export a PNG for screens or an SVG for print. Before publishing, scan the file with at least two different phones to confirm it resolves correctly at a realistic distance.

Tips for reliable scanning. Use a high-contrast palette (dark pattern on a light background), size the code for its viewing distance, and keep content short — more characters produce a denser pattern that older cameras struggle to read. Avoid covering the three corner squares with artwork or a logo.
Privacy reminder. A QR code displays whatever you encode. Avoid putting passwords, private contact details, or other sensitive information into a code you plan to print in public or share widely.

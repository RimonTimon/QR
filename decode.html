Check the destination carefully before opening. QR codes can contain phishing or unsafe links. CuteQR never opens a decoded link automatically.

Security note: Decoding only reveals the QR data; it does not verify that the content is trustworthy. Never enter passwords or payment details on a site just because a QR code points there.
🔒 Private by design

Image processing happens in your browser. No image upload, account, or decoder API is used.

🧩 Multiple recovery attempts

The decoder retries with resized, grayscale, contrast-adjusted, and rotated image variants when needed.

⚠️ Styled QR limitations

Illustrated, logo-covered, low-contrast, or damaged QR codes may not decode if too much of the data pattern is obscured. No decoder can guarantee every image.

Privacy & data

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to information you enter into CuteQR and what to consider when using third-party resources loaded by the page.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Information you enter

The QR generator is designed to create codes in your browser. The text, links, contact details, or Wi-Fi information you enter are processed by the page and its client-side QR library to generate the QR code. This project does not include its own application server or database for storing submitted QR content.

Third-party resources

The website loads front-end libraries from public content delivery networks (CDNs). When your browser requests those resources, the CDN or related service may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser information, request time, and requested resource. Those providers process information under their own policies.

Cookies and local storage

The current static project does not intentionally use a site-owned backend to collect QR content. Browser settings, extensions, hosting providers, or third-party resources may behave independently. See the Cookie Policy for more information.

Information security

Do not enter sensitive information into a QR code unless you understand the risks of sharing it. Anyone who can scan or access the generated code may be able to read the encoded content. On shared devices, clear the form and downloaded files when you are finished.

Children

CuteQR is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website changes. The “last updated” date above should be revised whenever substantive changes are made.

Contact

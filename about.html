About CuteQR

CuteQR helps you create customizable QR codes for everyday use with a simple, browser-based workflow.

What CuteQR does

CuteQR lets you create QR codes from content such as links, plain text, Wi-Fi details, email addresses, and phone numbers. Depending on the options available in the generator, you can customize the appearance and download the resulting code.

Designed to be simple

The generator is designed to work directly in your browser. The content you enter is processed by the page and its QR-code library; the site does not require you to create an account to use the generator.

Privacy note: The page loads third-party libraries from public CDNs. Those providers may receive standard connection information when your browser requests those resources.

Use QR codes thoughtfully

Always test a QR code before printing or sharing it. Avoid encoding sensitive information into a code that will be visible to other people.

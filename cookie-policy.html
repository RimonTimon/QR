Cookies & browser storage

Cookie Policy

This page describes cookies and similar technologies in the context of the current CuteQR static website.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies can include local storage, session storage, and other browser-based identifiers.

How CuteQR works

The current project is a static browser-based QR generator and does not include a site-owned backend or account system. The application is not designed to store the QR content you enter in a server-side profile.

Third-party services

CuteQR loads libraries from public CDNs. Those providers may use technical logs or other technologies according to their own practices. The exact behavior can depend on the service and its current configuration.

Managing cookies

You can review or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking some resources may affect how the website loads or functions.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated if the site adds analytics, advertising, accounts, or other services that change its use of cookies or similar technologies.
